I. THE AGENCY'S MANDATES & FUNCTIONS, NAMES OF ITS OFFICIALS WITH THEIR POSITIONS & DESIGNATION

The Department of Health CHD Caraga is committed to promoting openness and ensuring that policies, programs, standards, and essential health information are made available to support informed decision-making, strengthen public trust, and improve the delivery of quality healthcare services.

To fulfill this commitment, the Department carries out its mandate through the following key functional areas:

  1. Health Policies, Programs and Standards Development
  2. Health Information and Research Management
  3. Capacity Building
  4. Health Regulation
  5. Health Program M&E
  6. Technical Assistance
A. Vision | Mission | Goals | Core Values

VISION
Lead agency in health care system development for better health outcomes and well being of Caraganons.

MISSION
Committed to provide timely, responsive and sustainable support mechanism in a nourishing and enabling environment for quality health system to all, primarily the vulnerable sectors in partnership with various stakeholders in Caraga.

GOAL
Resilient health systems providing quality health care programs/services responsive to the needs of the populace in Caraga.

CORE VALUES
Surrounding STEWARDSHIP are six fundamental values that support and strengthen the agency's commitment to public health:
Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, Teamwork, Commitment, and Innovative.
